Home Comfort Engineered

Engineered For The Demands Of Houston Living.

Living along the Texas Gulf Coast places severe stress on residential HVAC systems. High ambient moisture, sudden thermal swings, and severe weather necessitate equipment that is properly charged, grounded, and maintained.

Humidity & Latent Heat Removal

An oversized or short-cycling system cools air too quickly without removing moisture, leaving rooms clammy. We match tonnage and airflow for thorough dehumidification.

Generator & Soft Start Readiness

During Texas grid disruptions and severe weather, soft start kits drastically reduce inrush starting amps on your A/C compressor, allowing systems to operate on portable or standby generators.

Attic Temperature Realities

Houston attics frequently exceed 130°F in July and August. We ensure air handler pans, insulation, and condensate drainage protect your ceilings from water leaks.
